Smart Travel Assistant - AWS Serverless Architecture

Event-driven corporate travel automation platform running on AWS Serverless (ap-southeast-2 Sydney), Slack Block Kit, and Multimodal Generative AI.

Tap any box or icon for what it does. On a phone, swipe the diagram sideways.

Architecture Flows
  1. Ticket Ingestion & Vision OCR: Traveler uploads flight ticket image in Slack. API Gateway acknowledges under 100ms and triggers async Slack Handler Lambda, which extracts itinerary via OpenRouter NVIDIA Multimodal Vision AI, stages a temporary draft in DynamoDB, and posts an interactive Block Kit preview card.
  2. Review, Modal Edit & Dual-Write: Traveler can click [Chỉnh sửa] to adjust details in a 14-field Slack modal form, or click [Xác nhận] to commit. The handler dual-writes the record into Amazon DynamoDB and Google Sheets (23 columns) with an idempotent SHA-256 row hash.
  3. 24h Flight Reminder & Sync: Amazon EventBridge triggers Flight Reminder Lambda daily at 08:00 AM VN. It queries DynamoDB for flights departing within 24h, prompts AI for packing tips and destination survival guide, posts alert to #biz-admin-alerts, and marks status SENT on both DynamoDB and Google Sheets.
